About SAFR

A regional alliance centered on first responder well-being

The Sunshine Alliance for First Responders brings peer ambassadors, first responder stakeholders, behavioral-health partners, and community organizations together around prevention and support.

Who We Are

Collaboration that reaches beyond any one agency

The Sunshine Alliance for First Responders (SAFR) is a network of peer ambassadors and first responder stakeholders. Its mission is to expand prevention efforts and promote the well-being of first responders and their families throughout Southwest Florida.

The alliance creates space for agencies and partners to identify needs, coordinate outreach, review ongoing efforts, and strengthen trauma-informed practices.

Our Purpose

Supporting the people who protect our communities

Prevention

Expand early intervention, awareness, and help-seeking before challenges become crises.

Well-Being

Promote mental health, resilience, and connection for first responders and their families.

Accountability

Encourage member organizations to use trauma-informed and supportive practices.

What the Committee Does

Turning partnership into coordinated action

  • Discuss needs within the first responder population
  • Develop outreach strategies
  • Review ongoing Hope Line efforts
  • Coordinate efforts among committee members
  • Promote trauma-informed practices among partner agencies
