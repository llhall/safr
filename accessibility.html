Accessibility

Accessibility statement

SAFR is committed to making its website usable for first responders, families, partners, and community members with diverse access needs.

Our approach

This website draft uses semantic headings, keyboard-accessible navigation, visible focus states, responsive layouts, descriptive alternative text, and strong color contrast.

Feedback

Once SAFR confirms its public contact information, visitors will be able to report an accessibility barrier through the Contact page.

Ongoing improvement

Content, forms, documents, and videos should be reviewed for accessibility before publication. Videos should include captions and transcripts, and downloadable documents should be created in accessible formats.
