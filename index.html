Who We Are

A collaborative network built for those who serve

The Suncoast Alliance for First Responders (SAFR) is a network of peer ambassadors and first responder stakeholders. Its mission is to expand prevention efforts and promote the well-being of first responders and their families throughout Southwest Florida.

Confidential Support

You do not have to face it alone.

Being a first responder means being dedicated to protecting others. Exposure to trauma and repeated challenges can leave you feeling overwhelmed, isolated, or as though no one understands.

You may put others’ lives first, but you can still make your mental health a priority. The First Responder Hope Line connects first responders with confidential support from trained specialists.

Call now 1-866-4FL-HERO

For immediate, confidential support.

Explore Help Options

Call or text 988 for immediate emotional or crisis support. Call 911 for a medical emergency.

What We Do

Prevention, connection, and support

01

First Responder Hope Line

Providing access to immediate and confidential support through 1-866-4FL-HERO.

02

Prevention & Outreach

Expanding prevention efforts, reducing stigma, and encouraging early help-seeking.

03

Alliance Collaboration

Connecting agencies, peer ambassadors, clinicians, advocates, and community organizations.

04

Trauma-Informed Practices

Encouraging participating agencies and organizations to recognize trauma and respond supportively.
