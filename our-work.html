Our Work

Strengthening prevention, access, and trauma-informed support

SAFR connects immediate support with broader alliance-building, outreach, and systems-level responsibility.

01

Direct Support

First Responder Hope Line

The Hope Line provides first responders with access to immediate, confidential support through 1-866-4FL-HERO.

View help options →
02

Prevention

Awareness and outreach

SAFR works to reduce stigma, promote early help-seeking, and expand prevention efforts across the first responder community.

03

Collaboration

Peer and stakeholder coordination

The alliance brings agencies, peer ambassadors, clinicians, advocates, and community organizations together to identify needs and coordinate efforts.

04

Systems Change

Trauma-informed practices

SAFR encourages participating organizations to recognize the effects of trauma and use practices that support first responders and their families.
