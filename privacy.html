Privacy

Draft privacy notice

This page must be finalized after SAFR confirms the organizations, vendors, and systems that will receive website information.

Pre-launch review required.

This draft is not legal advice and should not be treated as SAFR’s final privacy policy.

Information collected

The finished policy should explain what information is collected through contact, newsletter, and nonurgent-help forms; why it is collected; who receives it; how it is stored; and how long it is retained.

Third-party services

The finished policy should identify Constant Contact, any form provider, analytics tools, donation processors, hosting providers, and other third-party services used by the website.

Sensitive information

Health, crisis, and personal information require clear handling procedures. The nonurgent-help form should not be activated until SAFR confirms the privacy, security, and response process.
